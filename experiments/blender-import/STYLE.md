# Import probe style

A technical test asset, not Andy's room artwork. One checker-textured platform with an off-center rectangular opening and one raised block. Strong contrasting texture quadrants reveal flips and UV mapping. Distinct dimensions reveal axis or scale errors. Simple geometry keeps lighting and collision checks inexpensive.

Author geometry and texture in Blender. UDK owns final collision, compatible game material assignment and lighting. Keep the hole open when adding collision. Use a separate non-overlapping lightmap UV channel. Gravity -650, Z up. Preserve normal car physics and player settings.

Acceptance requires the actual saved map in Rocket League. Blender-only renders do not establish import, material, collision or lighting compatibility. The full room remains gated on this test.
